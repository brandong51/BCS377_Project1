About Me

Hello there, my name is Brandon Gaguancela. As I am writing this, I am a student attending Farmingdale State College. I am pursuing a degree in Computer Programming and Information Systems.

First before, I really get into it, I wanted to talk a little bit about myself. I come from a town near Farmingdale State College. I am first generation college student. Well, the fourth one in my family. Both of my parents are immigrants from Ecuador and they both wanted to pursue college so I can potentially get opportunities that they never got. Honestly when deciding where I wanted to go, I didn't want to get homesick and feel lost at new place. I was somewhat familiar with Farmingdale, both of my older siblings attended the college.

Throughout my time at Farmingdale State College, I studied programming, database design, system analysis, and computer security. And during my time at Farmingdale, I been able to used what I've learned and apply it to individual and group projects. When showing the projects that I have worked on throughout my time at Farmingdale State College, I will show some love to my fellow classmates and groupmates. I don't want to take all of the credit because I don't deserve all of it. It was truly an amazing opportunity to work with such great people and programming during my time at the college.
